3  Machine-room bearing reactions P1–P4
P1 to P4 are the vertical reactions at the four bearing seats of the machine chassis in the machine room. The total weight W of the suspended masses and the machine acts as one vertical load at the traction-sheave centre. W is shared between the front and rear bearing rows by their lever arms, and within each row between the left and right bearings by their lever arms.

P6 is the vertical reaction on the pit floor under the car buffers when the car with rated load strikes the buffers. The total acts on all car buffers together; the per-buffer value acts on each buffer.

7  Car guide-rail forces FF1 / FF2
FF1 and FF2 are the horizontal forces on the car guide rails from rated load distributed irregularly in the car. FF1 acts along the car-rail line, in the car-width direction. FF2 acts perpendicular to the car-rail line, in the car-depth direction. Both are forces per guide rail at one guide-shoe level.
h is the vertical centre-to-centre distance between the upper and lower car guide shoes. W and D are the internal car width and depth.
