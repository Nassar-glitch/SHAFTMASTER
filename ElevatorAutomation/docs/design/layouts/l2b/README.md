# L2b: the A0 geared sheet in the generated drawing (2026-10-12, fix round)

The AutoCAD adapter (`Elevator.AutoCAD/Sheet/SheetAssembler.cs`, called by the Core Console host after the four views are final) adds the
layout `SM_Geared_A0` of `Templates/Layout_Geared_A0.dxf` to the generated DWG, only when the generation request carries a `SheetRequest`
(the desktop always sends one). A request without it generates exactly what it did before.

| Step | What |
|---|---|
| Real extents | The AutoCAD geometric extents of each view's visible entities, dimensions and labels included (`GenerationResponse.Bounds`), plus a 100 mm margin (L2c; each title's drawn size, not its MText reference box) |
| Arrangement | `SheetArrangement` (Core): the shaft plan leads, no other main view takes a larger scale; a reference detail never takes a larger scale than its template scale, the door elevation is fixed at 1:20 |
| Layout | Cloned from the template (frame, `SM_TITLEBLOCK`, `SM_LOGO_AREA`); reference details cloned into model space at their offset (untagged) |
| Viewports | One per view, locked, standard scale, on layer `view port` (no plot); the detail viewports take 150 mm more on every side so an appended scale is not clipped |
| One title per view | The titles drawn in model space state the sheet scale: "SHAFT AND CAR 1:10", "MACHINE ROOM 1:10", "SEC AT A-A 1:50", "SEC AT B-B 1:20". The rail details get "1:10" appended to their title text. **No label under any viewport** (L2c). The titles print at 8 mm; the shaft plan, whose own title is hidden in the generated drawing, gets a visible "SHAFT AND CAR 1:N" added by the sheet; the details may be enlarged to 1:5 and state their real scale; the door elevation's geometry titles say 1:20 and the detail is fixed at it |
| Title block | `TitleBlockData.Attributes()`; Arabic in `SM_AR` (xarab), English in `SM_EN` (cent) |
| Attribute fit | Each attribute has a box (`Templates/Layout_Geared_A0.fields.json`, from the static geometry around it). A value is fitted to 92 % of the free width: width factor down to 0.7, then the height down to 55 %, then (company, owner, project) two lines in the attribute `<TAG>_2`, last resort a smaller height still |
| Logo | A linked raster image fitted in `SM_LOGO_AREA` |
| Plot settings | `DWG To PDF.pc3`, ISO full bleed A0, `monochrome.ctb`, layout, 1:1 (set when the device is available) |

## Fix round (Planner, 2026-10-12)

1. **Titles with the real scale** (above). Without a sheet request the drawings are unchanged (same entity counts and bounds, tested).
2. **No text on text in the title block.**
   - P1–P7 start 5 mm further left, so "P5 = 1360 KG." clears "IN CASE OF OPERATION OF THE" and the P6 / P7 bracket clears "KG.".
   - DATE and PHASE sit in the lower part of their cells, off their Arabic labels.
   - The signature names are held to 26 mm, clear of the "م." prefix.
3. **Long values fit their box.** The longest realistic values of every attribute are in the fifth test project (company 61 characters, owner
   in Arabic 74 characters, project 77 characters, 20 stops with all the stop names, long names and document number): none overflows. The
   company and the owner take two lines, the project one line at width factor 0.7.
4. **Drawing name** is a preset "لوحة الأعمال التنفيذية للمصعد" in the same title-block preset group (control, supply, type of work, drawing
   name), editable per project (General page, "Drawing title block"), written in the xarab style.

## What the check by eye found

Passed: no viewport overlaps another; one title per view with the real scale and no second label (the door elevation and the rail details
included); the title block is filled in every field; no value touches the static text or the frame (the long-values sheet is the worst case);
the Arabic company, owner, project, phase, drawing-name and person texts read correctly; P1–P7 are "P1 = 760 KG." as decided.

Open (not fixed here):

1. **The logo does not plot through the Core Console.** The image is in the DWG (`-IMAGE ?`: one definition, one instance, full path) but the
   plotted PDF has no image object. Options for L3: plot with the full AutoCAD, vectorise the logo, or put the PNG onto the PDF afterwards.
2. The company line is the first case where a long name takes two lines and the SHX text is measured by AutoCAD a few percent short of what
   plots: the fit uses 92 % of the free width. A font with real metrics would let this go.
3. The **fish plate detail** states no scale: its titles are flattened geometry that cannot be edited (the user's own edit deleted the label too).
4. A hamza on alef in the drawing name and in other Arabic text is lost where lam + alef-hamza is written as the plain lam-alef ligature
   (XARAB-CHECK: no glyph with the hamza joined to lam).
5. The 2S project fills 0.55 of the drawing area; the empty band lies centre-bottom, where the generated details of the later step can go.
6. The detail dimensions (`ISO-25`, `Standard` dimension styles) take the generated drawing's definition when the names clash; they look right
   in the five sheets but were not compared numerically.

## L2c (the user's own edit of the sheet)

The sheets above were re-plotted after L2c; see [../l2c/USER-EDIT-DIFF.md](../l2c/USER-EDIT-DIFF.md) for the rules taken from the user's edit.
B-B may stand above A-A and take 1:10; viewports follow the drawn extents; no scale labels; 8 mm titles; the unused rail-bracket variants
lose their label texts. In the 2S sheet the machine-room title (1:20) sits over the lower edge of the room outline: a placement of the
generated view's own title, not of the sheet.

### L2c, round 2: the column grid of the user's sheet

`SheetArrangement` now follows the composition of the user's own edit as a column grid (proportions of the drawing area; the arrangement
only picks the largest standard scale per view inside its cell and keeps the hierarchy rule):

- column 1: the machine-room plan above the shaft plan (space between);
- column 2: A-A at the bottom, the fish plate detail above it, centred;
- column 3: B-B flush top right (reaching left over column 2 when it is wider than column 3), under it the door detail, the guides and
  brackets plan and the rail-bracket elevation, top to bottom, right-aligned, space between.

The column widths follow the content (a tall A-A widens column 2, a wide shaft widens column 1); the slack is spread between the columns.
The reference details are cropped to what they draw (title with the scale included, rotated texts by their own size), margin 30 mm, so the
bracket elevation takes 1:5 as in his sheet. When the details do not fit their cells they are packed together in rows under B-B; only
when no grid combination exists at all does the earlier free packing run. The main views decide the scales first; the details break ties.
View titles are at most 800 model mm (8 mm printed up to 1:100). The user's project reproduces his scales and centres (layouts-native
case `user-sheet`: plan 1:10, machine room 1:10, B-B 1:10, A-A 1:50, door 1:20, guides 1:10, brackets 1:5; centres within 5 % of the
1189 mm sheet width). Fill and largest empty rectangle are measured by `tests/integration/sheet_fill.py`.

### L2c, round 3: titles

Every title on the sheet, of the views and of the details, is a real text that states the sheet scale and is 8 mm high on paper (model
height 8 mm x the scale denominator, recomputed when the scale changes); there is no separate "1:N" label anywhere. The titles that were
flattened glyph geometry in the template (door "VIEW at D-D" / "SEC. C-C", "GUIDE RAIL / FISH PLATE", "SIDE - GUIDE RAIL") are removed from
`Templates/Layout_Geared_A0.dxf` by `tools/layouts/build_template.py` and replaced by texts in the style of the other titles (cent font,
underlined); the guides-and-brackets and bracket-elevation titles are texts of the same kind with a wrap width (8 mm texts are wide).
The detail extents are measured at each candidate scale (the title is 8 mm at any scale, so its model size grows with the denominator).
Limit: from 1:150 a title at 8 mm would be 1200 mm high and 28 m wide (wider than the section and than the gap to the neighbouring
views), which the host rejects (views must not overlap): titles are 8 mm up to 1:100 and 800 model mm beyond (5.3 mm at 1:150; only
projects of 20 or more stops get there).
